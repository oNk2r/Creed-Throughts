Frequently Asked Questions (Classified)

What is Creed Thoughts?

In Season 3 Episode 24 - "The Job," Ryan creates a blog for Creed on The Office. Though initially it was a word document, it was later turned into a real blog with Creed's thoughts written in character by the show's writers.

Who is Creed Bratton?

Creed Bratton is a fictional character in The Office (US), played by the real-life musician Creed Bratton. He works in Quality Assurance at Dunder Mifflin Paper Company in Scranton, Pennsylvania, though no one is entirely sure what he actually does. He is known for his mysterious past and bizarre non-sequiturs.

Is this a real government website?

The URL "www.creedthoughts.gov.www\creedthoughts" is a reference to Ryan's comically incorrect understanding of how web addresses work when he set up the blog. It is not actually a government website. Do not attempt to contact any government agencies about this blog.

Who writes the posts?

The posts were written in character by the writers of The Office. They capture Creed's distinct voice — rambling, obliquely menacing, and full of casual references to a deeply suspicious past.

How many blog posts are there?

There are 7 pages worth of posts spanning from May 2007 through August 2008, documenting Creed's thoughts during and around the run of The Office Season 3 and beyond.
